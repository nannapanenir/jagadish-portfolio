How the Transflo modernization saved CSX about $1 million

System
Transflo Customer Inventory Management
My role
Java architect and modernization lead
Company
CSX Transportation
Outcome
About $1 million in cost savings

The starting point. Transflo's inventory system had grown up on older J2EE technology. Its dependencies made every change slow and kept maintenance expensive.

What changed. I set the target architecture and the route to it: removing legacy dependencies, consolidating the technology, and reshaping the application so it could scale and connect cleanly to the rest of the enterprise.

The result. Roughly $1 million saved, and a codebase the team can extend without fighting it.
